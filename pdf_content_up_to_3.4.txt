1 INTRODUCTION 
1.1 PROJECT OVERVIEW 
In 2015 a temperature logging system for the detection of overloaded transformers was piloted in KZN 
OU. The system was piloted as an improvement to thermal stickers which are an official Eskom 
recommended method of detecting overloaded transformers. However, as more thermal stickers are 
installed on transformer tanks, more effort is required to keep the transformer temperatures up to date as 
this requires CNC staff or Plant Management area staff to make frequent site visits to record or verify 
tank temperatures. The remote temperature logging system resolves these challenges by automating the 
data collection and analysis thus eliminating frequent transformer visits and hours spent analysing the 
collected data. 
 
1.2 DOCUMENT SCOPE/PURPOSE 
The Physical Architecture Definition (PAD)/Detailed Design contains content that is on a physical level of 
detail where it is needed for development purposes of the RTL Application.  
 
The PAD spans all relevant architecture domains (business, data & information, application and 
technology) and examines all relevant states of the architecture (baseline, transition, and target).
